The service flow

A practical route
for every batch.

FoodCare is a proposed neighbourhood collection point that combines clear records, local partners and human judgement.

  1. 01

    Drop off

    At closing time, a vendor brings unsold fruit or vegetables to the nearby FoodCare point.

  2. 02

    Weigh and QR-tag

    The operator records source and visible condition, weighs the batch and adds a QR label for simple traceability.

  3. 03

    Human check

    A trained operator makes the final routing decision. Software may support a suggestion, but it cannot certify food safety.

  4. 04

    Route to a local partner

    Suitable batches are directed to affordable basket sales, an appropriately licensed processor, or a composting partner.

  5. 05

    Learn and report

    Simple records show what was received, recovered, sold, processed and composted so the pilot can improve.
