The opportunity

Unsold does not always mean unusable.

By market closing time, sellers can be left with produce that is ripe, irregular, or simply hard to sell fast. Without a quick local option, its value can be lost along with the water, energy and work behind it.

One point, three thoughtful routes

Sort for value. Route with care.

FoodCare is not a donation app or a standalone machine. It is one low-cost local point that helps each batch take the safest, most useful next step.

The service flow

Simple enough to start local.

  1. 01

    Vendor drop-off

    Unsold produce arrives at a nearby collection point after market hours.

  2. 02

    Weigh & QR-tag

    The operator records source and visible condition, then adds a QR label.

  3. 03

    Human check

    A trained operator decides if and where a batch may be routed.

  4. 04

    Partner route

    The batch moves to resale, a licensed processor, or composting partner.

  5. 05

    Report impact

    The station records what was recovered, sold or composted.

Proposed business model

Value shared across the neighbourhood.

FoodCare is designed to be cost-conscious. The revenue ideas below are options for a pilot, not current income.

01

Modest transaction commission
A small share from affordable basket sales.

02

Market association service fee
A possible fee for better local waste handling.

03

Agreed processing margin
A transparent arrangement with processing partners.

Low-cost pilot plan

Begin with one market, not a big promise.

Test the station with five vendors and one collection point, using reusable crates, a weighing scale, QR stickers, a smartphone and local resale, processing and composting partners.
